## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:
• Better prices
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.
## Proposed Solution
1. Detailed Explanation of the Proposed Solution
Our solution, AgriConnect, is a digital platform that connects farmers directly with consumers, retailers, and bulk buyers without the need for multiple middlemen. Farmers can list their products, update their prices, and receive orders directly from buyers.
The platform will provide features such as product listings, fair price suggestions, order management, and delivery support. It will also help farmers find nearby buyers and allow consumers to purchase fresh agricultural products at reasonable prices.
2. How It Addresses the Problem
Currently, farmers often receive less money for their products because several intermediaries are involved in the selling process. At the same time, consumers have to pay higher prices.
AgriConnect helps solve this problem by reducing the number of intermediaries and allowing farmers and buyers to communicate directly. Farmers can earn a better profit, while consumers can buy fresh products at affordable prices.
3. Innovation and Uniqueness of the Solution
The unique feature of AgriConnect is that it brings farmers, consumers, and bulk buyers together on a single platform. Instead of simply acting as an online marketplace, it focuses on making agricultural trading fair, simple, and transparent.
The platform can suggest suitable prices based on market trends and help farmers find buyers in nearby areas. It can also support group orders from retailers and bulk buyers, making it easier for farmers to sell larger quantities of their products
## Technical Approach
1. Technologies to Be Used
AgriConnect will use modern and reliable technologies to create a simple digital marketplace for farmers and buyers.
- Frontend: HTML, CSS, and JavaScript will be used to design a user-friendly website.
- Backend: Python with Django will manage user registration, product listings, orders, and transactions.
- Database: MySQL will store farmer details, product information, prices, and order records.
- Price Recommendation: Basic data analysis will help suggest fair prices based on market trends.
- Location Services: GPS integration will help farmers find nearby buyers and reduce transportation costs.
- Payment Integration: Digital payment options will make transactions easier and more convenient.
2. Methodology and Process for Implementation
The development of AgriConnect will follow a step-by-step approach to ensure that the platform meets the needs of farmers and consumers.

Step 1: Requirement Analysis
Understand the challenges faced by farmers, such as low selling prices, lack of direct buyers, and difficulties in finding markets.
Step 2: System Design
Design a simple interface that allows farmers to upload products and buyers to search for agricultural products and compare prices.
Step 3: Development
Develop the frontend, backend, and database, and connect them to create a functional marketplace.
Step 4: Testing
Test the platform with sample users to ensure that registration, product listings, price recommendations, and order placement work correctly.
Step 5: Implementation
Introduce the platform to farmers and local buyers, collect their feedback, and improve the system based on their needs.

## Feasibility and Viability
1. Analysis of the Feasibility of the Idea:
AgriConnect is a practical and achievable solution that can help farmers sell their products directly to consumers and bulk buyers. The platform can be developed using existing technologies such as Python, Django, and MySQL, making it possible to build a working prototype with limited resources.
Farmers can use the platform through their smartphones, while buyers can easily browse products and place orders. By starting with a small group of farmers and local buyers, we can test the platform and gradually expand it to other areas.
The idea is both technically and economically feasible because it does not require expensive hardware or major infrastructure to get started.
2. Potential Challenges and Risks:
Some challenges may arise while developing and implementing AgriConnect:
- Limited digital knowledge: Some farmers may not be familiar with using online platforms.
- Internet connectivity: Farmers in rural areas may experience poor internet access.
- Trust and reliability: Farmers and buyers may hesitate to use a new platform without knowing whether it is reliable.
3. Strategies for Overcoming These Challenges:
To overcome these challenges, AgriConnect will focus on making the platform simple, reliable, and accessible.
- Easy-to-use interface: Provide a simple design with clear instructions and local language support so farmers can use the platform comfortably.
- Low internet usage: Develop a lightweight platform that works smoothly even with a slow internet connection.
- Building trust: Include verified farmer profiles, transparent pricing, and customer feedback to build confidence among users.

## Impact and Benefits
1. Potential Impact on the Target Audience:
AgriConnect aims to make a positive difference in the lives of farmers and consumers by creating a direct connection between them.
Farmers will have more opportunities to sell their products without depending on multiple intermediaries. This can help them receive better prices for their hard work and reach more customers.
Consumers will be able to purchase fresh agricultural products directly from farmers at reasonable prices. The platform will also help small farmers and local buyers connect easily, creating better opportunities for both.
2. Benefits of the Solution:
Economic Benefits:
